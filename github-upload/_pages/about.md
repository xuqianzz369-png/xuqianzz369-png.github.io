## About Qian

I am Qian Xu, an Assistant Professor of Practice at Iowa State University. My experience includes instructional design, university teaching, and professional learning, with work in online learning and educational technology.

I earned my Ph.D. in Curriculum and Instruction, with a major in Learning Design & Technology, from Purdue University in August 2025. I also hold a Qualitative Research Graduate Certificate (2024) and an M.S. in Curriculum and Instruction, with a major in Curriculum Studies (2019), from Purdue.

My applied experience includes learning design at Eli Lilly and Company and instructional design, consultation, and resource development at Purdue University.
